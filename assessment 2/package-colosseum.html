Colosseum & Forum Weekend

3‑night package • Central Rome • From AED 3,150 per person

What’s Included

  • Return flights from major airports in the United Arab Emirates to Rome
  • 3 nights in a 3★ or 4★ hotel near the historic centre
  • Guided Colosseum, Roman Forum, and Palatine Hill tour
  • Skip‑the‑line entry tickets for all included sites

Why You’ll Love It

This package is perfect if you want to focus on the most famous ruins of Ancient Rome. You’ll stand inside the Colosseum, walk the same streets as Roman senators in the Forum, and look down over the ruins from Palatine Hill — all starting from the United Arab Emirates.

Sample Itinerary

Day 1 – Departure & Arrival

Fly from the UAE to Rome, check into your hotel, and explore nearby piazzas and cafés.

Day 2 – Colosseum & Forum

Morning guided tour of the Colosseum, followed by the Roman Forum and Palatine Hill.

Day 3 – Free Day & Return

Free time to revisit your favourite ruins or explore museums before your flight back to the UAE.
