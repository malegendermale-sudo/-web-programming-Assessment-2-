Ruins of the Republic

4‑night package • Deeper ruins exploration • From AED 3,850 per person

What’s Included

  • Return flights from major airports in the United Arab Emirates to Rome
  • 4 nights in a central Rome hotel
  • In‑depth guided tour of the Roman Forum and Palatine Hill
  • Visit to lesser‑known ruins and archaeological sites

Why You’ll Love It

This package is designed for travellers from the UAE who want to go beyond the postcard view of Ancient Rome. You’ll spend more time in the Forum, explore ruins that many tourists miss, and get a stronger sense of how the Republic actually functioned.

Sample Itinerary

Day 1 – Departure & Arrival

Fly from the UAE to Rome, settle into your hotel, and enjoy an evening walk past illuminated ruins.

Day 2 – Forum & Palatine Hill

Full‑day guided exploration of the Forum and Palatine Hill with time for photos and questions.

Day 3–4 – Hidden Ruins

Visits to smaller archaeological sites, museums, and optional self‑guided walks before returning to the UAE.
